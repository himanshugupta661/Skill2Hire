How SKILL2HIRE Works

A two-sided trust ecosystem linking verified organizations with high-performing talent through AI analysis.

For Job Seekers

Accelerate your career journey without fear of ghost or fraudulent postings.

1
Create Account

Register with verified contact credentials and complete your professional bio.

2
Upload Resume

Our AI parses skills, education, tools, and experience directly into your structured profile.

3
Review AI Matches

Inspect similarity scores, missing skill alerts, and verified employer transparency stats.

4
1-Click Apply

Submit tailored applications and track real-time status updates directly from hiring managers.

For Employers & Recruiters

Hire top-tier talent backed by government-grade corporate verification.

1
Enterprise Verification

Submit CIN, GSTIN, and official domain records to earn the trusted Verified Employer Badge.

2
Publish Opportunities

Define required skills, salary spectrum, and work arrangements to instantly seed applicant pipelines.

3
AI-Ranked Candidates

Review incoming applicants ordered by matchmaking score, shortlist candidates, and schedule interviews.
