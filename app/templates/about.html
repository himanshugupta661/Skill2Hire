About SKILL2HIRE

Intelligent Employer–Job Seeker Matchmaking & Verified Employment Connections designed to eliminate fake job postings, credential fraud, and hiring bias.

Our Mission

Bridging Talents and Verified Employers with AI Precision

In the fast-evolving Indian employment landscape, job seekers battle fraudulent recruitment schemes and spam listings, while genuine employers struggle to sift through unqualified or misaligned applicants.

SKILL2HIRE solves this two-way trust barrier by combining rigorous employer background verification (CIN/GSTIN checks, physical office validation, credential trust score) with intelligent multi-dimensional matchmaking (TF-IDF vectorization, semantic skill extraction, weighted proximity, and salary alignment).
